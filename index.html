01 / THE FRAMEWORK

Three modes. One dynamic system.

CCF models consciousness through three interacting modes. These are conceptual and dynamical modes, not separate brain regions. Their relationships may help explain how experience shifts between grounding, destabilization, and integration.

01 / GROUNDING

PMC

Physical Mode of Consciousness

The mode of embodied presence, sensory information, and contact with the immediate physical environment.

02 / EXPLORATION

EMC

Entropic Mode of Consciousness

The mode associated with increased uncertainty and the loosening of established patterns, potentially opening space for new configurations.

03 / INTEGRATION

IMC

Integrative Mode of Consciousness

The mode proposed to bring distributed information into more coherent patterns of meaning, continuity, and organization.

AN EMERGENT CONFIGURATION

CCS — Coherent Consciousness State

Within CCF, a Coherent Consciousness State describes an emergent configuration in which interacting modes may become organized into a more integrated and coherent pattern.

02 / INFORMATION & DYNAMICS

From interaction to coherence.

CCF proposes that conscious-state organization depends not only on individual modes, but also on how information moves between them and how those interactions change over time.

01 / FLOW

Integrative Information Flow

IIF describes the proposed information-geometric landscape in which modes interact, transitions occur, and patterns of experience may become more or less stable.

02 / MEASUREMENT

Integrative Micro-Signals

IMS is a proposed multidimensional readout of the system's changing state, including modal dominance, flow-related curvature, integration, and temporal coherence.

03 / CHANGE

Nonlinear Dynamics

The mathematical model explores how coupling, inhibition, self-excitation, and stochastic fluctuations may influence transitions between different dynamical regimes.

CCF's proposed models and measures provide a foundation for ongoing mathematical investigation and empirical research.

03 / THE GEOMETRY OF TRANSFORMATION

The Hero's Journey through the lens of CCF.

CCF also explores a conceptual mapping between its account of changing conscious states and the Hero's Journey: a narrative pattern of departure, disruption, transformation, and return.

From established patterns to new coherence.

Familiar structures provide stability. Disruption can challenge those structures, opening a period of uncertainty and exploration. New patterns may then emerge as information is reorganized into a more coherent whole.

Through CCF, this narrative can be explored alongside the interaction of physical grounding, entropy, and integration. The mapping offers a way to compare a formal systems model with a longstanding pattern in human storytelling.

04 / RESEARCH & EXPLORATION

A framework to investigate, question, and refine.

CCF is intended as a theoretical scaffold for examining conscious-state organization and developing hypotheses that can be scrutinized through formal analysis and empirical work.

01 / THEORY

Formal Modeling

Examine mode interactions, stability, transition dynamics, and the mathematical conditions proposed to support integration.

02 / EMPIRICAL WORK

Measurement & Testing

Investigate whether candidate measures from neural, behavioral, and self-report data can meaningfully operationalize the framework's constructs.

03 / APPLICATIONS

Potential Applications

Explore possible relevance to contemplative practice, therapeutic research, complex systems, and artificial systems—without presuming that proposed applications have already been validated.

CCF is a developing theoretical framework open to continued investigation, testing, and refinement.

05 / THE INSTITUTE

Careful models. Open inquiry.

The Coherent Systems Institute is a research-oriented initiative exploring consciousness, coherence, and complex systems through conceptual models and formal inquiry.

Its aim is to develop ideas clearly, make assumptions visible, and create space for critical examination, refinement, and future research.
