01 / THE FRAMEWORK

Three modes. One dynamic system.

CCF models consciousness through three interacting modes. These are conceptual and dynamical modes, not separate brain regions. Their relationships may help explain how experience shifts between grounding, destabilization, and integration.

01 / GROUNDING

PMC

Physical Mode of Consciousness

The mode of embodied presence, sensory information, and contact with the immediate physical environment.

02 / EXPLORATION

EMC

Entropic Mode of Consciousness

The mode associated with increased uncertainty and the loosening of established patterns, potentially opening space for new configurations.

03 / INTEGRATION

IMC

Integrative Mode of Consciousness

The mode proposed to bring distributed information into more coherent patterns of meaning, continuity, and organization.

AN EMERGENT CONFIGURATION

CCS — Coherent Consciousness State

Within CCF, a Coherent Consciousness State describes an emergent configuration in which interacting modes may become organized into a more integrated and coherent pattern.

02 / INFORMATION & DYNAMICS

The dynamics behind coherence.

CCF proposes that conscious-state organization emerges not from individual modes in isolation, but from their interaction over time. The framework examines how information flows between modes, how system states change, and under what conditions more coherent patterns may emerge.

01 / FLOW

Integrative Information Flow

IIF describes the proposed information-geometric landscape through which the Physical, Entropic, and Integrative Modes interact. It provides a framework for examining state transitions, integration, and the relative stability of different patterns of system activity.

02 / MEASUREMENT

Integrative Micro-Signals

IMS refers to proposed indicators of changing system dynamics. These may include modal activity, temporal coordination, integration, and changes associated with information flow. Their operational definitions and empirical validity remain subjects of ongoing research.

03 / CHANGE

Nonlinear Dynamics

CCF models how coupling, inhibition, self-excitation, and stochastic fluctuations may shape transitions between dynamical states. Its central hypothesis is that the interaction of grounding and variability can support integration under particular conditions, rather than guaranteeing coherence in every case.

CCF connects a proposed dynamical architecture with a research program focused on mathematical refinement, measurable predictions, and independent empirical testing.

03 / THE GEOMETRY OF TRANSFORMATION

The Hero's Journey through the lens of CCF.

CCF explores a conceptual parallel between its proposed dynamics of conscious-state change and the Hero's Journey: a narrative pattern often described through departure, disruption, transformation, and return. This comparison offers a lens for exploration, not evidence that the two share a proven underlying structure.

From established patterns to possible reorganization.

Familiar structures can provide stability. Disruption may challenge those structures, creating a period of uncertainty and exploration. During this period, existing patterns may persist, break down, or reorganize into different forms of system activity.

Within CCF, this sequence can be considered alongside the proposed interaction of physical grounding, entropy, and integration. The Hero's Journey provides a narrative comparison for exploring these ideas, but the relationship between narrative stages and formal system dynamics remains a hypothesis requiring further theoretical clarification and empirical investigation.

04 / RESEARCH & EXPLORATION

A framework to investigate, question, and refine.

CCF is intended as a theoretical scaffold for examining conscious-state organization and developing hypotheses that can be scrutinized through formal analysis and empirical work.

01 / THEORY

Formal Modeling

Examine mode interactions, stability, transition dynamics, and the mathematical conditions proposed to support integration.

02 / EMPIRICAL WORK

Measurement & Testing

Investigate whether candidate measures from neural, behavioral, and self-report data can meaningfully operationalize the framework's constructs.

03 / APPLICATIONS

Potential Applications

Explore possible relevance to contemplative practice, therapeutic research, complex systems, and artificial systems—without presuming that proposed applications have already been validated.

CCF is a developing theoretical framework open to continued investigation, testing, and refinement.
