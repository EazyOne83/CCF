03 · THE HUMAN OS PROGRAM

A learning experience shaped around you.

The four-week Human OS Program uses a foundational training manual to guide learning, reflection, and practical exploration.

The program moves through four stages:

Week 1 · Grounding & Self-Awareness

Explore PMC · Physical Mode of Consciousness

Build awareness of your physical experience, attention, and connection to the present moment. Begin noticing your patterns without judging them.

Week 2 · Emotions & Uncertainty

Explore EMC · Entropic Mode of Consciousness

Examine how you experience uncertainty, emotional shifts, and disruption—and practice responding with greater awareness.

Week 3 · Perspective & Meaning

Explore IMC · Integrative Mode of Consciousness

Practice reframing experiences, considering different perspectives, and connecting ideas to develop a broader understanding of your experiences.

Week 4 · Integration & Coherence

Bring PMC, EMC, and IMC together

Explore how grounding, navigating uncertainty, and integrative perspective can work together. Develop a personal approach to applying the three modes in everyday life.

If you choose to enroll after a Discovery Session, your needs and goals will help guide how the foundational manual is adapted for your training.
